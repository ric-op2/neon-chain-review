# NEON CHAIN — Review

Browser review of NEON CHAIN v0.48, with thirty-four recordings. The newest clip shows visible full-gauge beat motion and restored song-menu text color. The v0.47 clip shows the rectangular high-score popup and clean background restoration after saving. The v0.46 clip shows the full-combo bonus, disk-save confirmation, and per-song high scores. The v0.45 clip shows restored craft outlines during right and left banks. The v0.44 clips show scrolling song selection, JUST turn sound A in RYZYYYN, and a stationary craft after CLEAR followed by replay. Older recordings retain their version labels. The spin-study page preserves the adopted A and comparison proposals.

Open the published page to compare five-bar meters, flight and exhaust, two-cue corners, and full EASY/HARD runs. Playback speed, frame stepping and local review notes are supported. Notes stay in the browser unless you copy or download them.

The videos contain NEON CHAIN visuals, the original tracks NEON AFTERGLOW and REDLINE MIRAGE, and RYZYYYN, developed from an automatic transcription of a user-provided OMY reference recording. The source recording and game disk are not included. This repository contains the review page and its media.
